# Headquarters and camp access

The shared web and native HQ menu follows the survivor from the abandoned camp to Level 4. The next milestone lists the required working buildings and materials. Buildings become available for on-site repair in pairs. The HQ also lists what each station can produce and which requirements the camp already meets.

See [Camp restoration](../CAMP_RESTORATION.md) for the four milestones, costs and unlocks.

## Upgrading

Visit the HQ planning bench to establish the camp or pay for the next level. Materials come from the backpack and shared camp stores. Repairing the last required building qualifies the camp for its next upgrade. The survivor still chooses when to spend the supplies at HQ.

The furnace can smelt iron once restored at Level 3. Supply wood and iron ore at the furnace, then take the resulting ingots to the restored workshop for metal equipment.

## Existing expeditions

Earlier expeditions could restore buildings in a different order. Loading an earlier save keeps its purchased facilities usable. Raid damage can disable a facility until repaired. Camp level persists through damage, travel and reloads.
