# Humans Remain — world master plan 01

**Current implementation, 15 October 2026:** the entire planned world now has 256 overlapping ground pieces sharing three warm/brown/clear-earth materials. A separate 42-section road layer connects all seven districts with camp stone, compacted earth, dark woodland earth, factory gravel and salvage gravel; see [the current road layout](world-road-layout.md) and `src/world-master-plan.ts`. Road materials and soil contacts blend gradually. The sector counts, partial bounds and upcoming terrain batches below describe the historical rollout and are superseded by the current implementation. Water, bridges and district scenery remain future work.

The original camp is the fixed anchor. The target is a connected 20,000 × 20,000 source-pixel world, about 17.4 times the area of its original 4,800 × 4,800 terrain. This is a production blueprint, not a claim that the full world is playable. The existing runtime still contains the original camp and 26 added terrain textures. Its current outer bounds are 11,508 × 6,206 source pixels, from x = −1528 and y = 0, with a concave footprint rather than a filled rectangle.

The owner requested a substantial open world resembling the supplied geographic reference, with connections on every side of camp, varied earth and grass, dirt roads of different widths, sparse leaf litter and substantial road-free ground. Build the surrounding ground in small verified batches. Outdoor connections remain in the same world; bunker interiors remain separate.

The owner's latest videos clarify the art separation: ground should stay basic, like camp. Use soil, subtle flat grass colour/texture and paths in terrain images; create height and density with independently placed foliage. Do not paint ferns, grass tufts, leaf rosettes, roots or plant shadows into the new backgrounds. Pieces 22–26 have a ground-only v2 correction under this rule. This is no additional land, and it does not claim all 21 earlier extensions are cleaned.

## Source of truth and coordinate contract

- `src/world-master-plan.ts`: district reservations, route graph, rivers, rail, road-free areas and the next adjoining batches.
- `src/world-plan-geometry.ts`: route contacts, connectivity and crossing checks.
- `/world-plan.html`: the public viewable plan, generated by `scripts/build-world-plan.mjs` during every build.
- `/planning/world-plan.json`: the same geometry, current built rectangles and batch contacts, for inspection and later tooling.
- `artwork-archive/world-guide/world-overview.jpeg`: the original geographic reference.

All plan coordinates are in the existing camp's source pixels. Source `(0,0)` is the northwest corner of the original 4,800-pixel camp image. World coordinates remain `(source − 600) × EAST_TERRAIN_SCALE`, with the same scale as the current game. The camp hearth is anchored at source `(2422.3919283440346,2268.9620145056724)`, taken from the revision-68 saved layout. Do not move, rescale or rewrite saved objects to fit the plan.

The planned bounds run from `(-7200,-6200)` to `(12800,13800)`. They must never replace `OUTDOOR_FOOTPRINT`, unlock player movement, change save validation, or become camera limits merely because they appear in this document. Finished, registered artwork defines the runtime footprint. Batches A1–A4 now expand those bounds southwest and west, while the original camp anchor remains unchanged.

## Routes and crossings

Built route centre lines are approximate surveys for design continuity. They do not replace painted paths, collision or actual contact pixels. Before generating a neighbouring patch, inspect the real adjoining image and fit the route's local bend to it. Planned route widths are source-pixel targets; routes can taper naturally without changing their destinations. Most future connections are dirt lanes or narrow footpaths. Preserve existing stone near camp, and use paving only where justified by established traffic.

The graph connects every district to camp. The northern loop links camp, the village and logging. The western loop gives a second logging approach and runs towards waterworks. The southern loop connects camp, the meadows, waterworks and the scrap district. The factory connects to the village and the existing works approaches. The graph's `access` edges only express movement within camp and are deliberately not drawn as roads through the hearth.

There are six designed road/river centre-line crossings, each assigned a bridge. Geometric checks prevent accidental additional centre-line crossings. They do not certify final bank clearance, bridge artwork, water collision or walking surfaces. River widths are provisional design widths. Before integrating water, reserve its banks, align bridge deck endpoints, and block water movement except at valid crossings. The current terrain rectangle union alone cannot provide river collision.

Five polygons explicitly exclude roads: deep western woodland, northern woodland, eastern hollows, southern open grassland and the already built quiet ground southeast of camp. Other areas may also remain road-free. Never add a path just because two terrain rectangles touch. Keep foliage and leaf litter local; use ordinary dirt or grass for broad stretches.

A batch can contain several close-up generation passes and several GPU textures. In particular A2, A5 and A6 must not be painted once at a low resolution and enlarged. Start from the actual composite of every neighbour, retain registered pixels through the contact feather and keep enlargement at or below about 1.1×. Inspect one source pixel per rendered pixel, including the middle of each patch. Reject pore rings, outlined pebbles, embossed dirt and repeated textures. Use lossless runtime textures with the existing sampling and fade rules.

## Integration and performance gates

The static deployment package is close to its size ceiling after this batch. Before adding A5–A6, move immutable terrain assets to the existing object-storage delivery path with verified hashes and caching, keeping versioned source art in git. Do not reduce source resolution or remove game/download features to make room. Four legacy gallery PNGs were losslessly recoded with exact decoded RGBA comparisons, old URLs redirected, and originals archived this release.

- Register only completed terrain in `OUTDOOR_SOURCE_TILES`. Keep loaded-ground readiness, exact footprint movement/build limits and save validation in agreement.
- The original camp's four textures currently remain resident; extra ground streams near the camera. A much larger world needs a texture budget and overview level of detail before whole-world camera views can safely request every high-resolution tile. This plan page uses a separate small overview and does not load the game's full terrain set.
- Keep interactive objects separate from ground images. As object counts grow, validate visibility/collision culling and simulation budgets rather than assuming terrain streaming is sufficient.
- Before each terrain publication, test adjacent seams in the production renderer, movement across all contacts, failed/slow downloads, and save/reload in the new area. Perform actual phone checks when the supported browser/device workflow is available.
- Update the small ground overview with `node scripts/render-world-plan-ground.mjs` when the installed terrain changes; rename the overview/version reference if needed. Then rebuild the planning page. Its highlighted footprint must always represent the actual runtime registry.

The original western release checks remain in `docs/qa/west-camp-v1`. The ground-only correction is checked separately in `docs/qa/west-camp-v2`: nine exact decoded contact comparisons, twenty production CanvasKit views, physical movement out and back, failed-download readiness, negative-coordinate save/read handlers and TypeScript/build validation. No browser or physical phone verification was available.
