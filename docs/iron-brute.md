The approved large orange tank robot joins the Scrap Walker, Scrap Bomber and Skitter. It has the reference's tiny recessed red visor, huge armoured fist, grapple claw and broad boots. It is a slow, durable threat with two committed special attacks.

## Capture correctness

The simulation moves the real player through collision-tested ground space; it never teleports them into the robot or through a wall. If cover blocks a cable/pull or the pull cannot progress, it releases immediately. Killing or clearing the captor, restoring health, player defeat, dawn and scene transfer all clear capture. Scene arrival cancels stale Brute attacks and gives fresh wind-up time. Escape/release grants a short damage grace period and 2.5 seconds of protection against immediate re-grabs.

The normal player draw is suppressed only while carried. The same player artwork is drawn once at the measured chest grip, then the actual painted forearms are redrawn in front. Rear-facing holds preserve depth occlusion. The character keeps their original dimensions; crushing uses arm poses and restrained recoil rather than resizing the player's head or body. The recessed visor cannot paint its glow through the held character's head.
