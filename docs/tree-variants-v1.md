# Tree variety

Two additional silhouettes for each of oak, pine, birch, deadwood, rowan and willow: 18 designs total. Generated with the built-in image generation tool using the approved species atlases as visual references. Original generated sheets are retained in artwork-archive/tree-variants-v1; the reproducible packer records the exact source filenames and measured registrations.

Art brief: preserve the approved crisp hand-painted leaf clusters, warm shaded bark, moss, upper-left lighting, elevated camera and maturity. Branching/forked designs add modest branch forks; bent/leaning designs alter the trunk and crown silhouette. Every design has a healthy tree, axe-notched trunk and matching stump. Production atlases share the healthy canopy and roots across damage states, replacing only a registered cut rectangle. Sources are not rescaled. Lossless WebP preserves alpha and visible RGB.

The one-time map pass changes 293 of the existing 441 trees within their original species groves. Density, ground anchors, collisions and placement settings stay intact. Owner edits and deletions win. The editor saves the migration through its existing authenticated save flow; Play also applies the same migration when loading the saved world.

Validation: typecheck; 441 timed axe sequences and manual log collections; 486 transformation/facing cases; 54 states rendered with the production CanvasKit adapter. Reports and state sheets are in docs/qa/tree-variants-v1. No physical-device FPS measurement or browser GPU test was performed.

The 12 new atlases total 8,808,708 bytes and 32.76 MiB decoded. Existing-image lossless conversions recover 9,923,007 transfer bytes, retaining original PNGs for native/source use and serving old web URLs through the existing redirect mechanism.
