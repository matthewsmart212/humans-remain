THE WAY OUT

A world built in connected stages.

  1. 01
    Ground foundation — complete

    The entire 20,000 × 20,000 terrain-pixel world is covered. The original camp stays fixed; surrounding ground uses large overlapping camp-style images with gradual colour blends.

  2. 02
    Western river — connected to the southern edge

    Twenty-six painted sections connect the northwest headwaters, logging bends, waterworks approach and southern reach. Four wooden crossings are walkable; water and rapids animate. The separate meadow channel and eastern creek remain planned.

  3. 03
    Northern village

    Connect both northern approaches, then build the district footprint and the woodland link back to the logging crossing.

  4. 04
    Factory and scrap districts

    Add separate eastern road overlays into the northern factory and southern scrap warehouse. Reserve the railway and building footprints first.

  5. 05
    Southern meadows

    Extend the southern trail, build the meadow crossings, and close the waterworks–meadow–scrap loop with substantial off-road ground.

  6. 06
    Dress and test the world

    Add separate foliage, buildings, resources and encounters; check movement, sightlines, return journeys and phone performance district by district.
