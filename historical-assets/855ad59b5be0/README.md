# Village approach — sparse ground

The final northern pair uses broad patches of bare brown dirt and muted grassy surface, with only occasional leaves. It preserves the warm contact strips next to the camp and woodland junction and keeps the larger dirt lane plus smaller eastern footpath.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The native image is 1705 × 922; it is registered to the camp coordinate scale and cut into two 1538 × 1538 lossless textures. Adjacent texture columns, the camp contact band and the junction contact rows match exactly after decoding.

The final prompt is in `generation-prompts.json`. The selected original is `generated-master-original.png`; `registered-master.png` retains the complete registered ground source. `terrain-manifest.json` records positions and seam checks. Earlier outpaint and palette prompts remain in `artwork-archive/village-approach-v1`.

Runtime files are `public/survival/maps/village-approach-v2/03-camp-east-clearing.webp` and `public/survival/maps/village-approach-v2/04-village-trail.webp`.

The current software-renderer evidence is in `docs/qa/village-approach-v2`. Browser and physical phone performance have not been tested in this environment.
